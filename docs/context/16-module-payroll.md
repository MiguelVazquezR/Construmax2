# Construmax2 ERP — 16: Payroll & HR Module (Recursos Humanos)

> **Business purpose:** Automate time & attendance and payroll pre-calculation for the company's collaborators: facial biometric kiosk, remote (geolocated) attendance, shifts, automatic lates/overtime, LFT mandatory rest days, incidents, vacations with weekly accrual, real-time pre-payroll, payroll periods with automatic closing, printable payslips and a mirrored expense in *Control de gastos*.
> **Context file covers:** Settings, payroll profiles, authorized devices + kiosk (face recognition), attendance punches, shifts and schedules, holidays, incidents, vacations, payroll periods and adjustments, payslips, the collaborator self-service portal (*Mi asistencia*), the automated artisan commands and the expenses integration.
> **Current scope:** Everything above is implemented. **Not included:** CFDI/SAT stamping, money disbursement, ISR/IMSS calculations, seventh-day/dominical premium, liveness detection (all noted as extension points).

## Business rules (implemented)

### Attendance & kiosk
- Kiosk page is public; **every** API call requires `X-Attendance-Device` matching an active `attendance_devices` row (token shown once, stored hashed). Unauthorized devices see a registration hint.
- The kiosk offers **two identification methods** (the collaborator picks one when both are available): facial recognition (live capture → Rekognition 1:N search → `identifier_method=face`, capture stored as evidence; when it is not enabled/configured the option is hidden) and the **kiosk pin alone** (`attendance.kiosk.punch` with only `pin` + type; the pin is matched through the `kiosk_pin_lookup` HMAC and verified against the bcrypt hash; pins saved before the lookup column existed are verified individually and upgraded on first use; a pin shared by two collaborators is rejected as ambiguous). The PIN option appears while `kiosk_pin_fallback_enabled` is on; when selected, the camera is **never started** (and fully released if it was on) and only the PIN field is shown — with both methods available the camera is requested only after the collaborator picks *Con mi rostro*. The notice *"El registro es personal e individual..."* to discourage punching for someone else is shown **only with the PIN method**, because face recognition identifies the collaborator directly.
- Duplicate punches of the same type within ±2 minutes are rejected.
- Remote punches (`/my-attendance`) require `can_remote_attendance`; geolocation is mandatory when `remote_geolocation_required`; when facial recognition is active and configured the photo is required and must match the authenticated user.
- Manual corrections stamp the audit columns; the reason is **optional** (when left blank, the reason stored by a previous correction is preserved).
- The period panel pre-selects the **recommended next punch** of the day (entry → lunch start → lunch end → exit) in the *Agregar registro* dialog and shows a hint with the suggestion; the same recommendation label travels in the day payload (`suggested_next`, computed by `AttendanceLog::nextSuggestedType`).
- Captures older than `attendance_capture_retention_months` are purged weekly by `payroll:prune-captures`.
- The **kiosk screen** follows the app theme and palette (brand orange `#f26c17`, system cards) instead of the old slate/black look: numbered flow (1 · choose the punch type, 2 · identify — face or PIN — where the camera only renders and starts for the face method), device chip in the header, camera card with its status notice and a clear success overlay with the suggested next punch.

### Schedules & attendance calculation
- Schedule resolution precedence: individual assignment → department assignment. Rotations advance one shift per completed week from `start_date`. Flexible shifts use `required_daily_hours`. **Per-day shifts** (`type = per_day`) take the start time, end time and meal minutes of the configured weekday (`day_schedules`), so a collaborator can work Monday–Friday 09:00–18:00 and Saturday 09:00–13:00 with its own meal time; days without an entry are rest days and the expected daily minutes are computed per day. The shift form explains **when to use each type** (`Shift::TYPE_DESCRIPTIONS`, exposed as `shiftTypeDescriptions`): an info box under the type selector — plus a tooltip on the type tag of the table — describes fixed, flexible and per-day shifts; fixed shifts show a live *Jornada* summary (start → end and net daily duration, meal included or excluded) and flexible ones the reminder that entry/exit times are not controlled and lates are not measured. The weekdays of fixed and flexible shifts are picked with compact day buttons. The **assignments tab** has a name search and a shift-type filter (both client-side over the loaded rows, with a *Mostrando X de Y asignaciones* counter and an empty state when nothing matches).
- The shift can be assigned right from the user/technician **forms**: the schedule selector (*Horario asignado*, `shift_id` field gated by `payroll.profiles.manage`) lives in the *Nómina* card, in the place of the removed «Horas por día» field, marked with the required asterisk while the collaborator is subject to payroll (external technicians do not render the block at all), and it carries an info icon that opens a summary popover with the worked days and their schedules — no need to open the shifts page. `AssignUserShiftAction` creates the individual assignment starting on the hire date (or updates the current one instead of duplicating it) and **keeps the payroll profile's daily hours aligned with the schedule** (`Shift::representativeDailyHours()`: fixed and per-day shifts derive them from their schedule net of unpaid meals — per-day averaged over its configured weekdays —, flexible ones use their required hours; guards keep profiles untouched when the shift is unknown or the hours are 0). Those hours feed the minute rate used by overtime and late discounts, so the calculations follow the schedule.
- The day summary is computed **live** (nothing materialized): worked minutes from the punch sequence, pauses (meal/break) subtracted unless `is_meal_paid`, late minutes beyond the shift tolerance (or global setting), early leave, overtime beyond the expected day. A worked **rest day** counts every worked minute as overtime (the same figure that feeds the weekly pool), so the *Extra* card and the per-day column of the period detail always add up to the collaborator total; worked holidays are excluded because they pay their own extra.
- Day status: `present`, `absent` (scheduled workday without punches **once the day has passed** → *Falta injustificada*), `no_record` (scheduled workday without punches whose date has not passed yet — today and future days → *Sin registro*), `rest_day` (the schedule does not cover that weekday → *Día de descanso*), `no_schedule`, `holiday` (worked holiday still counts for the extra pay), `incident` (fractional pay per incident type; IMSS incapacities pay 0 and are only counted for reference).
- Overrides (`late_ignored`) apply on top of the computed values.

### Holidays
- LFT art. 74 rules are generated by `payroll:sync-holidays` (idempotent; run yearly on Dec 1 and seeded for current+next year): Jan 1, first Monday of February, third Monday of March, May 1, Sep 16, third Monday of November, Dec 25 and Dec 1 every 6 years (from 2024). Manual days can be added/removed.

### Vacations
- LFT entitlement by year of service (12, 14, 16, 18, 20… +2 every 5 years). Seasons run from the hire anniversary; accrual is weekly (entitled ÷ 52 × completed weeks).
- **Vacations belong to the payroll flow**: the collaborator selector of the screen lists anyone subject to payroll **or** with attendance enabled (`VacationController::vacationUsers()`), so a collaborator without the attendance kiosk can still take vacations — managers can register the request on their behalf and self-service requests accept them too (`StoreVacationRequest`). `balanceFor()` also returns per-season `consumptions` (which request consumed the days of each season and when they were taken) plus `pool_consumptions`, so the user profile can show the season-to-year context (e.g. days of the 2024 season taken in 2026).
- Approved requests consume days **FIFO** from the oldest season; leftovers expire `vacation_carryover_months` after the season end (`addMonthsNoOverflow` semantics).
- Requests validate: minimum days (`vacation_min_days_to_request`), available balance, working days (rest days/holidays excluded) and overlap with blocking requests. Approvals create a linked `vacation` incident so payroll knows the days are paid.
- **Manual movements of the balance** (`vacation_adjustments`): the payroll team can register the *saldo inicial* of a collaborator, extra granted days and positive/negative corrections (one row per movement with days, reason, author and timestamp; type `initial` / `grant` / `adjustment`, only the adjustment accepts negative days). They **never expire**, they are consumed **after every season accrual** (LFT days are used first) and they also work when the profile has no hire date. They are registered from the user profile (*Vacaciones* card in *Información general*) and from the *Saldos por temporada* tab of the vacations screen; both screens list the movements with their author and allow deleting them (the balance is always calculated live, so deleting the row recalculates it). `VacationService::balanceFor()` exposes `adjustment_days` (net) and `adjustment_available_days` (remaining pool after consumption).

### Payroll periods & payslips
- **Weekly periods, always Monday to Sunday**: the type is fixed by the system (the settings form no longer offers *Tipo de periodo* nor the anchor date). The automatic rollover closes the week that just ended (**Sunday 23:59**) and opens the current week (**Monday 00:00**): `payroll:close-period` is scheduled on both moments and the same idempotent sync also runs when the periods screen loads, so a missed cron catches up on the next visit. **Two periods can never share dates**: opening a period rejects any range that repeats existing dates or overlaps an open one, and the *Abrir siguiente periodo* confirmation shows the exact dates that will be opened (the first full week after the last period). The *Abrir siguiente periodo* button is only offered when the last period was **closed early** (a manual close before its last day leaves the remaining days unmanaged): the automatic rollover opens the following week by itself, so in the normal flow the button stays hidden. Several periods may stay open at once (for example two consecutive weeks) as long as their dates differ.
- Closing a period early is allowed — the confirmation dialog warns that the remaining days stay uncovered until a reopen or the next Monday — and it still freezes one payslip per payroll subject (snapshot + lines + days), creates the **mirrored expense** in *Control de gastos* (total net, configurable category) and notifies `payroll.period-closed` subscribers. **A period reopened by hand (`reopened_at`) is never closed automatically again**: it stays open until the manager closes it. `--period=` forces the manual close of a single period.
- Reopening deletes the frozen payslips and the linked expense — blocked when the expense was paid; the expense cannot be deleted or marked paid from the expenses module while linked.
- **Receipts no longer require closing the period**: `PayslipService::printPayloads()` returns the frozen payslips for a closed period and the live pre-payroll (same numbers as the on-screen rows) for an open one. The print page marks those slips as *Pre-nómina* (no folio + a preliminary note). In *Periodos nómina* the `Recibos` button prints every collaborator and each row has a `Recibo` button that prints a single one (`users[]`). The page **does not open the print dialog automatically** anymore — the toolbar carries *Imprimir / Guardar PDF* — and its *Volver* button (same for the *Cerrar* of the pre-nómina sheet) closes the tab when the page was opened from the period screen, otherwise walks the history back or returns to the period detail as a last resort.
- **Pre-nómina sheet** (`Pre-nómina` button in the period header): `PayslipService::prePayrollPayloads()` (same builder as the print payloads) plus the period comments and incidents feed `Payroll/Periods/PrePayroll.vue`, a printable A4 landscape **summary** — one row per collaborator with days to pay (and unpaid days), the incidents of the period (type, date range, days with/without pay and notes) **together with the holiday days paid by law** (listed as `Día festivo` with the holiday name and a `trabajado (pago extra)` hint when the day was worked; they count in the incidents counters) and the comments (author + timestamp), a totals row with the head count, and KPI cards (collaborators, days to pay, incidents, comments). It has **no expandable detail** (the full detail lives in the period screen) and it is **light-only**: the page removes the dark theme while it is open and restores the user preference when leaving. The amounts (percepciones, deducciones, neto) are intentionally not listed; a footnote points to the period detail.
- **Manage every collaborator from the period screen**: the period page keeps the KPI cards and shows one collapsible panel per collaborator (`Components/Payroll/EmployeePeriodPanel.vue`) with their own tabs. The collapse header carries an initials avatar, the name/employee data and the summary (days paid/unpaid, lates, overtime, adjustments, incident/comment counters and the net amount in a highlighted pill) — vacation and incapacity days are not shown in the header on purpose (the client asked to see them only inside the *Días y registros* / *Incidencias* tabs); `Expandir todos` / `Contraer todos` open or close them in bulk, and the detail is fetched per panel on first expansion (`payroll.periods.days`). The **Días y registros** tab is a full-width table — day (weekday, date, shift and the day notes under it) — **days without a single punch (absences, vacations, holidays, rest days, pending days...) collapse into one full-width status pill** across the data columns, keeping the date, the shift and the ⋮ menu visible (the *Estatus* column was removed and *Asistió* is never shown: having records already means attendance). Days with records show separate columns — **Entrada** (time of the first punch; when the day has a late, the minutes beyond the tolerance are shown below in red, strikethrough and grey while ignored), **Salida**, **Tiempo de comida** (lunch duration, tooltip with the range) and **Tiempo trabajado** (worked vs expected time plus the record count as a link that opens a per-day dialog with every record: its time, type — entrada/salida/comida/permiso —, source, device, face/PIN and edited flag with its audit reason, plus the evidence photo, Google Maps link and edit/delete actions, and an `Agregar registro` shortcut that pre-fills the selected day — the add/edit dialogs use **separate date and time inputs**) — plus the overtime column and a **⋮ actions menu on every day**: *Editar registros* (opens the records dialog; *Ver registros* for viewers), *Quitar retardo* / *Restaurar retardo* (the late override that used to be an inline checkbox, now with the late shown on the *Entrada* column) and the incident options — when the day already has an incident the menu swaps to *Modificar incidencia* / *Quitar incidencia*, so an overlapping one can never be registered from there. The table is preceded by four range cards (days shown, time worked, lates, overtime). Evidence (photo/map) is only available inside the per-day records dialog. Status tags (and the full-width pills) are **color-coded by type** (the live day payload exposes `incident_type_key`): green for present days, holidays and **rest days**, amber for vacations and unpaid permissions, blue for justified absences and paid permissions, red for unjustified absences (past workdays without a record) and neutral for pending days (today and future workdays without a record → *Sin registro*), days without schedule and medical leaves; the incidents tab and the pre-payroll sheet reuse the same palette. **Horario semanal** shows the current week (shift, schedule, expected hours, workday tag); **Incidencias** lists the period incidents with type tag, dates, pay behaviour, notes and receipt/delete. The same panel keeps the audited per-punch actions, incidents register/delete, manual adjustments, the comments thread (author avatars, inline edit/delete) and that collaborator's receipt.
- **Date filter**: a `Filtrar por fecha` range picker (limited to the period dates) narrows the days and punches of **every** collaborator — the panels re-fetch with `from` / `to` and `Ver todo el periodo` restores the full range. Beside it, a **collaborator filter** (searchable multi-select with tags, `collapse-tags-tooltip`; technicians end with a `Técnico` tag) and a **type filter** (*Administrativos* / *Técnicos*) show only the matching panels and open them automatically. The **KPI cards follow those two filters**: with nothing selected they show the period totals, otherwise they recalculate over the filtered subset (a note reminds *"Las tarjetas reflejan únicamente a los colaboradores filtrados"*). The selection also travels to the `Recibos` and `Pre-nómina` buttons as a `users[]` query parameter (`PayrollPeriodController@prePayroll` and `PayslipController@print` accept an array or a comma-separated list), so both pages print **only the selected collaborators**; with no selection they include everyone. Technicians show `Técnico` as their position everywhere the rows are rendered (period header, pre-payroll, receipt snapshot). The header carries **previous/next period** buttons (the `previousPeriod` / `nextPeriod` props) so periods can be browsed without going back to the index.
- **Incidents stay editable on closed periods**: the *Incidencias* tab of every collaborator panel accepts adding, editing and deleting incidents even when the period is closed (the frozen payslips keep their figures until a *Reabrir periodo*; punch corrections and adjustments remain limited to open periods). The tab rows carry *Editar* (the same dialog used to add, pre-loaded) next to *Comprobante* (only when the incident has a support file) and *Eliminar* — the *Editar hora* shortcut was removed on purpose: the punch times are only edited from the *Días y registros* tab. **Two approved incidents can never cover the same day for the same collaborator**: the store and update requests share the `EnsuresNoIncidentOverlap` rule (message *«Ya existe una incidencia en uno de esos días para este colaborador.»*), the update endpoint (`PUT /payroll/incidents/{incident}`) recomputes `days` and excludes itself from the check, and the day menu hides the add options when the day already has an incident.
- **Alphabetical order**: collaborators are always listed by name in every payroll screen — period rows/panels, pre-payroll sheet and receipts — including the frozen payslips of closed periods (`payrollSubjects()` already ordered by name; the closed-period rows and payloads are sorted after fetching).
- **Terminology**: attendance punches are labeled *registro(s)* everywhere in the UI and in the flash/validation messages (buttons, columns, dialogs, kiosk copy); the old *marcaje* wording was fully replaced.
- **Comments per collaborator** (`payroll_notes`): added at the bottom of each collaborator panel, with author and timestamp, editable and removable in place by anyone with `payroll.periods.manage`. They are informational (they never change the pre-payroll numbers) and they also travel to the pre-payroll sheet (dedicated *Comentarios* column with the full text).
- Calculation notes: daily salary basis; overtime (including every minute worked on a rest day) is paid **at the normal rate** as a single *Tiempo extra* line (the old double/triple split was removed; the minutes still land in the `overtime_double_minutes` column so legacy sums keep working); worked holidays pay an extra × multiplier; incidents (vacations and paid permissions pay full days; IMSS incapacities are counted but never paid; justified absences, unpaid permissions and unjustified absences are not paid) pay their fraction on scheduled workdays and **also when the collaborator has no schedule assigned**, whose registered incidents are the only source of truth for those days — so vacations count as paid days for collaborators without an assigned shift (they add to days paid and, when the type is `vacation`, to the vacation days); lates only discount money in `deduct_minutes` mode; no ISR/IMSS. A scheduled workday without punches **only counts as an unpaid day once the day has passed** (unjustified absence): today and future days stay *sin registro* and count as neither paid nor unpaid, so an open period never anticipates absences.
- Print view uses browser print (`@media print`, `@page A4` margins) → "Save as PDF". Receipts (`Payslips/Print.vue`) are modern **auto-height** cards: header with folio and period, the employee fields (name, number, position, department, daily salary), percepciones/deducciones columns with subtotals, the attendance summary (days paid and **Faltas** — the days that were not paid because the collaborator did not attend —, lates, overtime, vacations, incapacity), a totals box with the net highlighted in the brand colour and a signature line (`Recibí de conformidad`, plus the preliminary note for open periods). The height adapts to the content (text never gets clipped) and `break-inside: avoid` keeps every receipt whole across pages. The receipts page is also **light-only** (same dark-theme removal as the pre-payroll sheet); the person profile photo uploader (`Components/Forms/PhotoUploader.vue`) is shared by the user and technician forms, and the payroll/attendance fields (`Components/Payroll/PayrollProfileFields.vue`) are grouped in **Colaborador / Nómina / Asistencia** cards with switch tiles and helper texts.
- Payroll subjects: `PayrollCalculatorService::payrollSubjects($onDate)` returns the collaborators whose profile is still valid on that date (`PayrollProfile::scopePayrollSubjectOn`): from `hire_date` up to and including `termination_date`. The period screens pass the period start date, so a collaborator dismissed before the period disappears from the rows, the pre-payroll and the payslips, while the historical periods keep their data.
- Employment window inside a period: `calculateFor()` skips the days before `hire_date` and stops at `termination_date`, so the days after the dismissal are never counted as absences or paid days. The period rows and the pre-payroll show a `Baja dd/mm` badge when the collaborator was dismissed inside the period.
- Dismissed collaborators cannot register attendance either: `RegisterAttendancePunchAction` rejects kiosk (PIN and face) and remote punches dated after the termination date, `StoreAttendanceLogRequest` rejects manual punches after it (corrections on or before that date still work) and the *Mi asistencia* portal shows a notice with the punch form hidden (`profile.can_punch`). Their history, vacations and receipts remain available.

### Faces (AWS Rekognition)
- `FaceRecognitionService` contract with an AWS implementation (CreateCollection/IndexFaces/SearchFacesByImage/DeleteFaces) and a null implementation; bound in `AppServiceProvider` depending on `services.aws` credentials (`AWS_ACCESS_KEY_ID`, `AWS_SECRET_ACCESS_KEY`, `AWS_DEFAULT_REGION`, collection name via `PAYROLL_REKOGNITION_COLLECTION` or `AWS_REKOGNITION_COLLECTION_ID`).
- Enrollment replaces previous active references (up to 3 photos, quality stored). Admin flow from Users/Show; self-service from *Mi asistencia*. Kiosk and remote punches use the search result.
- The **profile photo** uploaded in the Users **and Technicians** forms acts as the face reference when the module is active (`EnrollProfilePhotoAction`, invoked from `UserController` and `TechnicianController`): the portrait is indexed with `external_image_id = user id` and replaces the previous references only after a successful detection — a photo without a detectable face leaves the old references untouched and the flash message reports the outcome. Collaborators that already have photos can be indexed in bulk with `php artisan payroll:enroll-profile-photos --force` (requires `face_recognition_enabled` + credentials and the collaborator's "Registra asistencia" flag). The live-capture dialog (up to 3 photos) remains available for corrections.

## Profile screens (Users / Technicians → Show)

- **Información general** merges the general data and the former *Nómina y asistencia* tab: a "Datos generales" field-card grid followed by a "Nómina y asistencia" section (employee number, hire/termination, **horario**, salary, hours, kiosk PIN status, subject flags and face enrollment). There is no separate payroll tab anymore.
- The **technicians show** screen repeats that payroll summary inside the *Perfil 360°* tab (employee number, hire date, horario, salary, hours, kiosk PIN status and the three subject flags, with an empty state when there is no profile). `TechnicianController@show` sends the profile loaded through `user.payrollProfile` plus `currentShift`.
- The **kiosk PIN** field is part of the payroll/attendance block in the Users and Technicians forms (digits only, 4 to 12; an empty value keeps the stored pin). It lets each collaborator register attendance **with their PIN alone** (no employee number needed) when they prefer not to use their face.
- The **horario** card shows the shift resolved for today by `ScheduleResolverService` (controller sends `currentShift` in `UserController@show`) and a `ShiftSummaryPopover` (info icon) with the read-only detail: worked days, start/end, flexible hours or per-day schedules, and meal time.
- The vacation season collapse blends with the card (no darker panel): transparent `--el-collapse-*` background variables set in the scoped style.

## Notes / gotchas

- HTTPS is required in production for camera and geolocation (localhost exempt). The kiosk is face-only, so the kiosk device needs a working camera (the PIN endpoints remain API-level only).
- The AWS binding falls back to the null provider when credentials are missing; the UI shows warnings and enrollment/punches return validation errors in that state.
- SQLite (test env) stores date casts as strings: always use `whereDate` in scopes and validation closures for uniqueness.
- Carbon 3 returns signed diffs (`abs()` needed) and `addMonths` overflows — use `addMonthsNoOverflow` for vacation expiry.
- `expenses.created_by` is nullable; the expenses UI renders "Sistema" for command-generated expenses.
- **Dark mode with Element Plus tables**: do not hardcode header backgrounds; scoped `:global(.dark) :deep(...)` selectors do not reliably apply in this codebase. Prefer transparent backgrounds plus EP variables (`--el-table-header-bg-color: transparent`, `th` transparent, text via `var(--el-text-color-secondary)`), which work in both light and dark mode. Same pattern for the vacation pending-row tint (low-alpha background).
- Out of scope (quote): CFDI/SAT, money disbursement, ISR/IMSS, seventh-day premium, liveness detection.
